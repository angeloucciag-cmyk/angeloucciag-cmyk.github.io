Multi-Robot Cooperative SLAM & Exploration

Two TurtleBot3 robots explore an unknown environment together in Gazebo. Each runs its own SLAM and Nav2 stack, and their local maps are merged into one global map.

  • Namespaced multi-robot setup: separate topics, TF trees and Nav2 stacks
  • Frontier-based autonomous exploration (explore_lite) per robot
  • Custom laser filter so robots don't map each other as obstacles
  • Map merging with known and unknown initial poses, with fixes contributed to m-explore-ros2

MATLAB · Robotics Toolbox · 2025-26

PD Control with Gravity Compensation — PUMA 560

Operational-space PD controller with gravity compensation that holds the PUMA 560 at a desired pose with maximum payload (2.5 kg) under a −90 N external disturbance, with realistic sensor and actuator models.

  • Operating pose chosen by maximizing the Yoshikawa manipulability index
  • Gain tuning from operational-space inertia Λ(q) for mass-spring-damper behavior
  • Euler ZYZ vs axis-angle: avoids representation singularities
  • Motor currents verified within the real actuator limits

MATLAB · Robotics System Toolbox · 2024-25

Obstacle Avoidance with Niryo Ned2 using CHOMP

Pick-and-place motion planning for the Niryo Ned2: CHOMP turns minimum-jerk initial guesses into smooth, collision-free joint trajectories around a shelf. The project then stress-tests the limits of its sphere-based collision model.

  • Workspace analysis with the Yoshikawa index to pick and place targets
  • Covariant gradient-descent optimization of smoothness + obstacle cost
  • Shows how sphere over-approximation turns feasible motions into collisions
